FAQ

Common questions about ArslanAI Video Maker.

Is ArslanAI Video Maker really free?

Yes. The story, script, and storyboard generator is free to use. Real video rendering may require free API credits from third-party AI video tools.

Can it create 30-minute videos?

It can generate a full 30-minute movie plan, script, scenes, and prompts. Rendering a 30-minute video requires connecting an AI video API or editing the scenes in a free editor like Shotcut or DaVinci Resolve.

Does it work for YouTube Shorts and Reels?

Yes. Choose 9:16 aspect ratio and 30–60 seconds duration for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.

Can I use the generated script commercially?

You own your ideas. Always check the license of any third-party AI tool you use for final video generation.
